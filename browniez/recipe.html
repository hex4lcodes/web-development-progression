Deep-Dish Brownies

Biz McMahon's all-time favorite brownies (made-from-scratch recipe), thanks Granny McMahon <3

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups white sugar
  • 3/4 cup butter, melted
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat oven to 350 degrees F (175 C) - Grease 8-inch square pan
  2. Blend sugar, melted butter and vanila into a large bowl. Beat in eggs one at a time, mix well after each addition
  3. Combine flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt into a medium bowl. Gradually mix into egg mixture until combined
  4. Spread into prepared pan
  5. Bake in preheated oven until brownies begin pulling away from the sides of the pan (40-45 minutes)
  6. Let cool then cut into squares
